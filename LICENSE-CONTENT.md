<div align="justify">

Three Harmonics is a reading journal. The quotes it collects are **not** the property of the author of this site. Each quote belongs to the person, character, studio, or publisher who originated it.

</div>

**How quotes are used:**

- Reproduced as **short quotations** — for commentary, criticism, and reflection. Uses typically permitted under fair-use and fair-dealing doctrines.
- Each quote carries an **in-line attribution** — the author's name, or the character and film where applicable — alongside a source chip: **🎬 Film** · **🔬 Science** · **📖 Literature**.
- Where an attribution is contested, disputed, or widely-attributed but undocumented, the accompanying reflection says so explicitly.

**The journal does not claim ownership of any quoted material.** If you are a rightsholder and would like a quote removed or re-attributed, please open an issue on this repository.

## 📋 How to Attribute

If you quote an essay in your own writing, use a format like this:

> In his essay "Small Moves, Long Run" (Three Harmonics, 2026), Pravinkumar Ghodake writes that *"the impossible is not a state — it is an undecomposed problem."*
>
> Retrieved from [pravinkumar-ghodake.github.io/three-harmonics/day-02.html](https://pravinkumar-ghodake.github.io/three-harmonics/day-02.html)

**A name, a title, a link, and a year.** That's the whole requirement.
